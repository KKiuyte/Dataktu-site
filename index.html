About dataktu

dataktu is a French-language data-journalism channel. Each short video (30–40 seconds, vertical 1080×1920) answers a question raised by the news with one verified figure from public data — national statistics offices, open-data portals, scientific publications — and always cites its source.

How dataktu uses TikTok

dataktu runs a private, single-user publishing tool. It is used only by the dataktu publisher, only on the dataktu TikTok account, which he owns. No other TikTok user ever signs in to it, and it is not offered to the public.

  1. Sign in with TikTok. The publisher authorizes the tool once through TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE).
  2. Upload. When a video is finished and approved by the publisher, the tool sends it to the dataktu account with the Content Posting API. The publisher then reviews it in the TikTok app, adds the caption and publishes it himself.
  3. Measure. Once a day the tool reads the account's public statistics (followers, views, likes, comments and shares per video) to compare which subjects work best.
